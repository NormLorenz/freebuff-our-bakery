Our Story

A family oven, shared with the block

Crumb & Craft started in 2009 with one secondhand deck oven and a stubborn belief that good bread should be a ten-minute walk from home. Linda and Norm still open the shop themselves most mornings.

We mill a portion of our flour, we compost every scrap, and we give leftover loaves to the community fridge on the corner at closing time.

  • 🌾 Flour from a family mill two counties over
  • 🧈 Real butter, never shortcuts
  • ♻️ Leftovers donated nightly, zero waste by 2027
